Emma has always believed that no one should have to go through difficult times alone.

Over the years, she’s proudly supported some incredible charities, using her voice, time and community to raise money, awareness and spirits.

Child Bereavement UK

Emma was a long-standing volunteer with Child Bereavement UK, supporting their work in the community. Through a four-week Saturday programme, she helped grieving children through creative activities, reading together and gently talking about the special person they had lost. The programme gave children and their families vital support at one of the hardest times in their lives, and helped them begin to rebuild.

In 2019, to mark the charity’s 25th anniversary, Emma took on the Alpine Challenge: hiking three mountains, in three countries (Italy, Switzerland and France), in three days. Together, the group raised an incredible £178,000 for the charity.

Bury Cancer Support

For five years, Emma volunteered as an in-house counsellor at Bury Cancer Support Centre, running weekly and sometimes twice-weekly sessions. As a qualified person-centred counsellor, she offered a safe space to talk and helped patients, families and carers work through bereavement, health worries and relationships.

Alongside her counselling, Emma helped bring together the Stock Exchange Hotel and the Tim Bacon Foundation for Dream the Impossible II: The Sequel. The evening was part of a wider initiative supporting seven cancer charities, including Bury Cancer Support Centre, and raised an incredible £17,465.77 for the centre. She continues to support the charity with its events today.

Destination Florida

Emma has been a long-standing supporter of Destination Florida, helping to run three years of the charity’s Ladies Lunch at The Lowry Hotel in Manchester.

In 2023, she joined the charity’s eight-day trip to Florida. Alongside a team of nurses, doctors and physios, Emma helped take 74 seriously ill children on a truly magical adventure.

For eight days, hospitals were swapped for theme parks, sunshine and fun. It gave the children the chance to simply be children, make friends for life, and create memories they’ll treasure forever.

Moya Cole Hospice

Emma and her husband Gary are passionate supporters of Moya Cole Hospice (formerly St Ann’s Hospice). In 2024, they co-hosted the very first Great Mancunian Ball at Aviva Studios, bringing people from across Manchester together for an unforgettable night. The evening raised an incredible £565,980 towards the hospice’s brand-new building in Heald Green.

Emma also hosted the Great Mancunian Lunch, raising a further £40,000 for the new hospice’s Being You Centre. Plans are already underway for the second Great Mancunian Ball in 2027, with an ambitious target of £750,000.
