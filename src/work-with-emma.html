Emma Neville is the founder of This is Me, a person-centred counsellor, menopause coach, speaker, philanthropist and passionate advocate for women’s health and wellbeing. With a lifelong passion for helping others Emma first qualified as a DIP Counsellor in 2019. Emma experienced a diagnosis of Premature Ovarian Insufficiency (POI) at 40. Determined to better understand her own experience and support other women, she went on to qualify as a MSCC Accredited Menopause Coach in 2023.

Inspired by conversations with women navigating similar challenges, Emma founded This is Me, a community dedicated to connection, conversations and breaking down the rhetoric surrounding menopause and women’s health. Since launching, the community has grown to more than 40,000 people. Emma works alongside worldwide organisations and women’s health communities. With continuous learning through her menopause coaching accreditation, Emma is supporting people through life’s transitions so everyone can be seen, heard and validated.

In 2025, Emma was appointed as UA92’s first-ever Women’s Health Ambassador. Since then, she has hosted awareness sessions, workshops and programmes for students, staff and industry partners, helping to open up conversations about women’s health. She also contributed to the university’s first women’s health policy, helping to shape the support available to everyone at UA92.

Selfridges Chat for Change: World Menopause Month

Emma was invited to bring her Menopause & Me talk to Selfridges Chat for Change to celebrate World Menopause Month.

Held in their Manchester City Centre store in San Carlo Cicchetti restaurant, Emma brought her two hour experience to their VSPs (Very Selfridges People!).

“Through a quiz, Emma’s personal story and key takeaways, attendees left feeling more connected, more informed and reminded they’re not alone.”

“Emma brought authenticity, warmth and expertise to the conversation, sharing her lived experience in a way that was both relatable and impactful. Her contributions were thoughtful and considered, and she demonstrated a real understanding of the audience, balancing personal insight with practical advice and guidance that attendees could take away and apply.

What stood out most was Emma’s ability to create a safe and inclusive space for discussion. She spoke openly and honestly about a topic that can often feel difficult to navigate, helping to normalise important conversations and encourage others to share their experiences.

The feedback from attendees was incredibly positive, and her contribution played a key role in the success of the event.”

Sharlene John, Head of DE&I, Recruitment and Onboarding, Selfridges

Betfred x This is Me Experience Virtual for World Menopause Month

To highlight and celebrate World Menopause Month, Betfred partnered with Emma to deliver the This is Me Experience Virtual to colleagues across multiple offices.

Through Emma’s personal storytelling, evidence-based insights, and warm, engaging presentation style, attendees gained a deeper understanding of menopause, wider women’s health topics, and the practical ways they can support colleagues in the workplace, all within their lunch hour.

“Emma was a dream to work with. She brought real warmth and expertise to our World Menopause Day session at Betfred, helping to create an open and informed conversation around menopause in the workplace.

Her personal story was powerful, and she balanced this with practical guidance and clear advice that colleagues could take away and reflect on. Emma’s knowledge of the subject really came through, but so did her ability to make people feel comfortable engaging with a topic that can sometimes feel difficult to talk about.

The session received a really positive response from our colleagues. I’d happily recommend Emma and This Is Me to any organisation looking to raise awareness, support colleagues and encourage more open conversations around menopause at work.”

Callum Astley, Internal Communications Lead, Betfred

“Real Stories, Real Impact” Panel Discussion with JD and The King’s Trust

Emma has built a longstanding partnership with JD Sports and was invited to join a panel discussion at a women’s health breakfast hosted by The JD Foundation and The King’s Trust, centred around the theme “Real Stories, Real Impact.”

Joining Jeanette Jackson, radio host, nutritionist and author, Paralympian Carly Tait, and Phoebe from CoppaFeel!, Emma shared her personal experience of navigating Premature Ovarian Insufficiency (POI) and early menopause. Through honest conversation and lived experience, she encouraged women to seek support, build connections, and feel empowered to thrive through life’s transitions.

“Emma’s knowledge and enthusiasm for the topic was engaging, honest, personal and heavily resonated with many of our colleagues. The feedback was incredible with women really connecting and having meaningful conversations on the day and beyond.”

“I would highly recommend Emma for workplace events.”

Claire Lindley, Group Head of Social Impact, JD Sports

This is Me Beliefs

After being in the room and hearing so many stories that relate to so many of life’s eventualities, sometimes we simply just need to listen. We don’t always have to have the answers.

Empower

We believe that all women are powerful and capable. We empower women by providing them with the knowledge, resources, and support they need to make informed decisions about their health and wellbeing, build confidence and resilience, and achieve their goals.

Support

We offer a safe space for women to talk about their experiences, receive emotional support, and learn coping mechanisms in a non-judgemental environment. We connect like-minded women to feel less alone, validated and not isolated.

Community

We believe that everyone has a story to tell. This community supports each other as we learn from one another to feel less alone, lift each other up and have a network to lean on through all stages of women’s health and beyond.

Empathy

Empathy is at the heart of everything we do. Behind every story is a person and when people truly feel seen and heard that’s when you start to really evolve to be authentically you.
