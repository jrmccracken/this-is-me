This is Me

I’m Emma Neville, a Manchester-based qualified menopause coach, person-centred counsellor, philanthropist, speaker, and mum of two teenage daughters as well as a proud Pisces and massive Take That fan! Experiencing Premature Ovarian Insufficiency (POI) at 40, which was a huge shock that left me with a lot of denial due to my age, brought about a real interest in my future health and what the word menopause meant for me. With having two girls, it was paramount I was informed and educated to pass on my knowledge to them.

I’ve turned my pain into passion to raise awareness for the 40+ year old woman because I realised just how many more of me there is out there. I created my This is Me online community to raise awareness around menopause so the future generations don’t suffer in silence.

After educating myself and gaining an accreditation as an MSCC Menopause Coach, I now share my lived experience whether its online, on the streets of Manchester interviewing the general public speaking on a podcast and my biggest joy, going into organisations to help them support their workplace in gaining an understanding on menopause and women’s health.

Let’s make menopause matter today and everyday.
