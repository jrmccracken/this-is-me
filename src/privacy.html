Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and look after your personal information when you visit this website, contact us or sign up to our newsletter.

Who we are

This website is run by Emma Neville, trading as This is Me (“This is Me”, “we”, “us”). We are the data controller for the personal information described in this policy.

If you have any questions about this policy or how we use your information, email us at [EMAIL ADDRESS].

The information we collect

When you contact us

If you use our contact form, we collect your name, organisation (if you give it), email address and anything you include in your message.

When you sign up to our newsletter

We collect your email address and a record of your agreement to receive our newsletter, including the date and the page you signed up on.

When you browse our website

If you accept cookies, we collect information about how you use the site, such as the pages you visit, how you arrived at the site, the type of device and browser you use, and your approximate location (based on your IP address). If you have also contacted us or signed up to the newsletter, this browsing information may be linked to your details. If you decline cookies, we don’t collect this information.

Our website host also keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the date and time of your visit) to keep the site secure and working properly.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss workplace sessions, events, panels or collaborations you’ve asked about.
  • To send you our newsletter and other updates from This is Me, if you have signed up to receive them.
  • To understand how our website is used, so we can improve it, if you have accepted cookies.
  • To keep our website secure and prevent misuse.

We will never sell your personal information.

Our lawful basis

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), we rely on:

  • Consent for sending you our newsletter and for using analytics cookies. You can withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests for responding to enquiries you send us and for keeping our website secure.
  • Contract where your enquiry leads to us working together.

Please don’t include health information in our contact form unless you’re happy for us to see it. If you choose to share it, we will only use it to respond to you and will treat it with extra care.

Who we share your information with

We use trusted service providers to run this website and our communications. They only process your information on our instructions:

  • HubSpot, which we use to manage enquiries, store newsletter sign-ups, send emails, run our cookie banner and measure website traffic. Our HubSpot account is hosted in HubSpot’s EU data centre.
  • Netlify, which hosts this website and may keep a backup copy of contact form submissions.
  • Google Analytics, which we use, only if you accept analytics cookies, to understand how visitors find and use the site. IP addresses are not stored.

These companies are based in the United States and may access or process information there. Where this happens, your information is protected by appropriate safeguards recognised under UK law, such as the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to standard contractual clauses.

We may also share information if we are required to by law.

How long we keep your information

  • Enquiries: up to 2 years after our last contact with you, or longer if we go on to work together and need to keep records for legal or accounting reasons.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use your information;
  • ask for your information in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, for example by using the unsubscribe link in any of our emails.

To use any of these rights, email [EMAIL ADDRESS]. We will reply within one month.

Keeping your information safe

We use reputable providers with strong security, limit who can access your information, and only keep it for as long as we need it.

Children

This website and our newsletter are intended for adults. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

Complaints

If you’re unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to put things right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK’s data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
