I’m proud to call myself a Mancunian, so when Lisa Morton asked me to join her on We Built This City, it was an easy yes.

We talked about all of me: Emma before menopause, during menopause and after menopause. Three different versions of me that have all led to who I am today.

We went from Robbie leaving Take That to my diagnosis of Premature Ovarian Insufficiency at 40, something I never saw coming and did not know how to make sense of at the time.

Manchester is filled with incredible people, and I’m on a mission to make menopause matter for women across Greater Manchester and beyond.
