Many women spend all day putting everybody else first: work, children and partners, often above themselves.

When I turned 40, I went through early menopause. It was a shock. I had assumed menopause happened later in life and had not connected brain fog or hair loss with it.

It took menopause to make me realise I had to prioritise myself so I could be my best for everybody else. That is why I created This is Me.

After three years, the community is more than 40,000 strong. This October, we are celebrating with an afternoon marking Menopause Awareness Day, hearing from incredible women and making time for ourselves.
