If you’ve been reading my emails for a while, you’ll know I love a good book recommendation. There’s nothing quite like getting lost in a book that makes you think, feel or laugh.

## A Book That Moved Me

### A Year of Nothing by Emma Gannon

I read this whole book on a 13-hour flight to Kuala Lumpur. I couldn’t put it down. I found it moving to see how such simple steps helped Emma on the road to recovering from burnout.

## A Book to Educate

### Navigating Menopause After Cancer by Dani Binnington

Dani shines a much-needed light on menopause after cancer. Having worked in a cancer centre as a counsellor for five years, this topic is close to my heart.

## A Book to Inspire

### Start With Yourself by Emma Grede

I love Emma’s upbeat, take-no-nonsense attitude. Her framework is about getting out of your own way so you can achieve your goals.

## A Book for Fun

### Underbelly by Anna Whitehouse

This was recommended as a purely enjoyable read. Anna looks beneath the filtered surface of modern women’s lives to uncover the darker truth underneath.

## The Books I Keep Coming Back To

### Let Them by Mel Robbins

A lesson in letting people think what they think, make their own choices and freeing up that energy for yourself.

### The Untethered Soul by Michael A. Singer

I return to this book because it brings me peace. It helps you question the stories you tell yourself and recognise how much power you have over your own mind.

### I Still Believe in Miracles by Lucas Jones

Lucas’s poems are helping me put words on paper again. There is so much meaning in poetry when you really feel the words.
