## Before your appointment

- If you need more than the standard appointment time, ask whether you can book a double appointment.
- Ask whether your surgery has a doctor with a special interest in menopause or whether a referral may be appropriate.
- Take a notepad and consider bringing somebody with you if you feel nervous.
- Write down how you have been feeling, both mentally and physically.
- List any medication you currently take.
- If you are considering HRT, do some research beforehand and prepare your questions.

### Things to note about your cycle

Changes in flow, length, pain, pain during or after sex, missed periods, mood changes, changes to the colour of blood, and the first and last day of your period.

### After your appointment

If you are not satisfied, remember that you can ask to speak to another GP.
