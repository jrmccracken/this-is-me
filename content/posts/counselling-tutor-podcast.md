If you had told me ten years ago that I would be on this podcast, I would not have believed it.

While training as a counsellor in my thirties, I listened to Rory Lees-Oakes for CPD. I was delighted to join him and share what I have learned since being diagnosed with Premature Ovarian Insufficiency at 40.

Counselling skills such as reflection, being comfortable with silence, open-ended questions, observation and empathy have helped me through every phase of life. They also made me think about what it would have meant to have a client like me sitting across the room.
