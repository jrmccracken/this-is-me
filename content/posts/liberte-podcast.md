Losing confidence at work could definitely be classed as a menopause symptom. It may not be listed alongside night sweats and brain fog, but many women experience it.

When I received my Premature Ovarian Insufficiency diagnosis, my work and confidence took a hit, alongside trying to keep my mum hat on and remember everyone’s activities.

I joined Rachel Peru on Liberté - Free to Be to share my experience of rebuilding confidence. If you are struggling with confidence while trying to build your career, this conversation is for you.
